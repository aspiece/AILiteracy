Step 1: Get Ready to Prompt

Use the 5-Tip Formula to write your first prompt for your career choice.

💡 5-Tip Formula Reference

Be Specific: Tell the chatbot exactly what you want to know.

Give the chatbot a role: Give it a job (e.g., "You are a mechanic").

Define the output: Tell the AI exactly what you need (e.g., a list or a story).

Ask the chatbot to ask questions: Tell the AI to ask you questions to learn more about what you need.

Have a conversation: Talk to the AI like you are talking to a friend. Be direct and clear.
