Step 2.1 · Start here

AI chatbots can draft, explain, organize, and revise information quickly. They generate language by predicting likely words and patterns, so a response can sound polished without being accurate, complete, fair, safe, or appropriate.

In this lesson, you will use the 5-Tip Prompting Formula to guide a chatbot. Then you will use the 3-Step Process to check the full result. You will decide why AI is being used, who may be affected, what proof and safety steps are needed, and whether to use, revise, or reject the result.

⚖️ Ethical Evaluation Lens

Before approving a chatbot-supported task, ask:

  • Purpose and reason: What problem needs to be solved? Why is AI being considered, and what benefit is expected?
  • Task limits: What may the chatbot help with? What should it never decide or do?

Learn: Write Stronger AI Prompts

Estimated time: 7 minutes

Responsible AI Routine: Goal → Protect → Use → Check → Own

📖 Read: How to Like a Pro

AI is powerful, but it is only as good as the instructions it receives. A is a question or set of instructions that tells an what to do. Use these 5 techniques from the video to get better results:

  • Be Specific: Include precise details so the AI can focus its answers on exactly what you need.
  • Give it a Role: Assign a persona or perspective (e.g., "Act as a marine biologist" or "Act as a high school student") to shape the tone and context.
  • Define the Format: State clearly how you want the answer organized (e.g., a list of 10 items, a single sentence, or a 2,000-word story).
  • Tell it to Ask You Questions: Instruct the AI to ask you clarifying questions first to gather details before giving its final answer.
  • Have a Conversation: Talk to the AI directly and clearly as you would with a friend. If your first attempt isn't perfect, keep the conversation going with follow-up instructions.

💡 Key Takeaway: Prompting takes thought and experimentation. Don't stress over your first —you can always refine the conversation as you go!

Learn: Check and Improve AI Responses

Estimated time: 7 minutes

Responsible AI Routine: Goal → Protect → Use → Check → Own

📖 Read: Check and Improve AI Responses

Getting great results from AI is rarely a one-step process. Just like human writers, AI chatbots rarely produce a perfect final draft on the first try. Use these key strategies to review and refine AI responses:

  • for Accuracy: Make sure the AI followed every direction. Check the facts with another trusted source because AI can make believable mistakes.
  • Focus on what to do: Tell the AI what it should add, fix, or explain. Use clear and positive directions. For example, say "Include examples with clear steps" instead of "Don't make it generic".
  • Ask for Sources & Reasoning: Ask why the chatbot gave its answer and where the information came from. Then check its logic and facts.
  • Maintain Human Ownership: Revise and edit the so that it reflects your unique creativity and judgment. You are responsible for the quality and accuracy of the finished work.

💡 Key Takeaway: Working with AI takes thought and persistence. AI gives you a helpful starting point, but humans must always check, fix, and approve the final work!
